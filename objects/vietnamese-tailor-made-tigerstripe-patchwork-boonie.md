## Object Details

* Tailor-made from scraps of different patterned tigerstripe camouflage material.
* Crown top cut as a single oval panel; crown band and short brim each pieced together from multiple scraps.
* Brim stitched with concentric rows of quilting and finished with a bound edge.
* Partial black cotton lining in the crown, now heavily faded; no sweatband, maker's label, or size marking.
* No ventilation grommets or foliage band.
* Heavy wear: small holes and tears, fraying at the seams, and fading that has turned the black stripes on the outside of the brim purple.

### Provenance

* Purchased by the author in the late 1990s from *Indochina Militaria*, a mail-order catalog published by dealer John O'Connor.
* No attribution to a wearer or unit accompanied the hat; its history before that sale is unknown.
