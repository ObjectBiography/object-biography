## Object Details

### M42 Jump Jacket
* WWII U.S. Army M42 paratrooper jump jacket
* Airborne rigger-modified with gray British duck canvas reinforcements at elbows and pocket gussets
* Reinforcement style associated with the 82nd Airborne Division
* Laundry identification stamped inside: "H 0608"
* Evidence of removed officer rank insignia on shoulder epaulets

### Veteran
* Technician Fourth Grade (T/4)
* 507th Parachute Infantry Regiment
* Headquarters Company, 504th Parachute Infantry Regiment
* Company L, 156th Infantry
* Service dates: November 25, 1940 – September 23, 1945

### Campaign Participation
* Sicily
* Naples-Foggia
* Rome-Arno
* Rhineland
* Ardennes
* Central Europe

### Decorations
* Bronze Star Medal
* Good Conduct Medal
* American Defense Service Medal
* American Campaign Medal
* European-African-Middle Eastern Campaign Medal with Silver Service Star
* World War II Victory Medal
* Combat Infantryman Badge
* Bronze Arrowhead
* Honorable Service Lapel Button

### Provenance
* Acquired from a fellow collector in July 2007
* Laundry identification researched against an 82nd Airborne personnel database containing approximately 43,000 names
* Identification corroborated through National Archives records, recorded honorable discharge, Social Security Death Index, and obituary
* Army Serial Number matched the laundry identification stamped inside the jacket
* Jacket attributed to the identified veteran; direct ownership history prior to acquisition remains undocumented
