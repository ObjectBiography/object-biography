## Object Details

### DCU Top
* Six-color desert camouflage ("chocolate chip") uniform top
* Interior label with 1997 contract number SP0100-97-D-C811
* Embroidered Naval Special Warfare Trident and U.S. NAVY branch tape, right breast pocket
* Collar rank device at collar point
* Name tape above left breast pocket, covered
* Acquired in the Virginia Beach, Virginia, area

### Attributed Veteran
* Enlisted 1987; completed BUD/S 1988; SEAL Team Two, including Desert Shield/Desert Storm
* Selected for DEVGRU in 1993; served there nearly 19 years
* Deployments included Haiti, Bosnia, Somalia, Yemen, Afghanistan, and Iraq
* Later served as DEVGRU Command Master Chief
* In 1997, the date of this top's contract, he was a chief petty officer four years into DEVGRU
