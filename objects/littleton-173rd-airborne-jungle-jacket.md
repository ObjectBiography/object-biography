## Object Details

* Coat, Man's, Combat, Tropical, in OG-107 cotton rip-stop poplin; contract DSA 100-69-C-1363 (1969), Burgess Mfg. Inc.
* Size Small Regular (chest 33–37 inches, height 67–71 inches), stock no. 8405-935-4705.
* All insignia theater-made:
  * 173rd Airborne Brigade shoulder sleeve insignia on both sleeves, believed to be Okinawa-made, though this cannot be confirmed. They are fully machine-embroidered but thinner than U.S.-made patches, thin enough to roll easily between the fingers.
  * Vietnamese-made subdued Combat Infantryman Badge, subdued parachutist wings, "LITTLETON" name tape, and "U.S. ARMY" tape.
* A stain on the lower right pocket that looks like rust or blood, permeating through all layers of the fabric.

### Provenance

* Purchased by the author in the 1990s from a vintage clothing exporter that sourced garments from rag mills and other sources and shipped them to Japan.
* The veteran was never identified.
* The author sold the jacket to a friend in the early 2000s. Years later, the author asked to buy it back, and the jacket returned to the collection.

### Additional History

* Anyone with information about the soldier named Littleton who served with the 173rd Airborne Brigade in Vietnam, or who may have served alongside him, is encouraged to [contact Object Biography](/contact/). Photographs, recollections, and additional context would be greatly appreciated in helping identify him and preserve his service history.
