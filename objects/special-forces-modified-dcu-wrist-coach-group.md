## Grouping Details

### Modified DCU Top
* Three-color Desert Camouflage Uniform (DCU) top, 1999 contract
* Lower front pockets relocated to sleeves
* Hook-and-loop fields added for removable insignia, name and branch tapes, and infrared flag
* "MOD 2" handwritten on interior size tag
* Farsi name tape embroidered in cinnamon thread on tan nylon webbing, tentatively translated as "Hall"

### Wrist Coach
* Black elastic wrist coach with clear vinyl windows and hook-and-loop closure
* Handwritten operational reference cards on repurposed printer paper
* Contains radio channel assignments, callsigns, football-themed brevity codes, and a numbered reference grid
* One sheet bears portions of a printed 7th Special Forces Group beret flash, Distinctive Unit Insignia, and "UNCLASSIFIED" marking
* Original mission, date, and operator unidentified

### Provenance
* Badged uniform and wrist coach found together at a military surplus store in Fort Walton Beach, Florida
* Association with 7th Special Forces Group suggested by the accompanying materials and acquisition location
* Original ownership and service use unconfirmed
