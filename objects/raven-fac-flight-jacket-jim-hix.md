## Object Details

### L-2B Flight Jacket
* U.S. Air Force L-2B light-zone flight jacket, size Medium
* Manufactured by Alpha Industries, 1970
* Specification MIL-J-7448J; contract DSA 100-70-C-1312
* Sage green nylon shell with orange lining and knit collar, cuffs, and waistband
* U.S. Air Force pilot wings, parachutist badge, and "HIX" name tape on left chest
* Theater-made, hand-embroidered Raven FAC patch on sleeve, marked "LAOS / 1,000 MISSIONS / RAVEN / FAC"
* Evidence of removed organizational insignia on right chest and officer rank insignia on shoulders
* Heavy wear to Raven patch embroidery; otherwise well-preserved

### Historical Association
* Attributed to James "Butch" Hix Jr. (1944–2014), U.S. Air Force pilot and Raven Forward Air Controller
* Served with the Ravens in Laos during the Vietnam War, including at Long Tieng and Pakse
* Shot down over Laos on February 9, 1971, and rescued by Air America
* Awarded the Purple Heart and four Distinguished Flying Crosses, according to his Air Force Academy memorial
* Period photograph shows Hix wearing an L-2B flight jacket during his Raven service, believed to be this example.

### Provenance
* Acquired from a vintage clothing dealer before its planned sale into the Japanese market
* Identification supported by the "HIX" name tape, Raven insignia, and documented service history
* Direct ownership and service use of this particular jacket remain unconfirmed
