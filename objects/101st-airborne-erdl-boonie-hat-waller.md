## Object Details

* Vietnamese theater-made boonie hat constructed from ERDL camouflage fabric.
* Full-color, merrowed-edge 101st Airborne Division insignia sewn to the crown.
* Theater-made, subdued parachutist wings.
* "WALLER" and "NEBRASKA" embroidered directly onto opposite sides of the brim.

### Provenance

* Attributed to Jimmy Dwayne Waller (1949–2016), born in Nebraska City, Nebraska.
* Served in the U.S. Army from October 1967 to October 1970.
* His obituary, published in the Lincoln Journal Star, confirms service with the 101st Airborne Division during the Vietnam War.
* Hat surfaced during the liquidation of Waller's estate following his death in April 2016.
* Acquired by the author from another collector in 2017; the intervening chain of custody is undocumented.
