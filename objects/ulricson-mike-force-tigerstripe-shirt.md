## Object Details

* Late war lightweight tigerstripe camouflage shirt.
* Size "US-M" rubber stamp inside the collar.
* Machine-sewn, theater-made "ULRICSON" name tape and "MIKE FORCE" tape in matching late war lightweight tigerstripe material.
* South Vietnamese silk woven jump wings above the name tape.
* Hand-embroidered U.S. jump wings on late war lightweight tigerstripe above the Mike Force tape.
* A folded OD handkerchief remains inside the pocket.

## Service

* Enlisted: PFC, Training Group, July 1967 – June 1968.
* Spec 4, 6th Special Forces Group (Airborne), June 1968 – November 1968.
* Spec 4 – SSG, 5th Special Forces Group (Airborne), December 1968 – March 1970, including six months with the Rhade of the 1st Mobile Strike Force.
* Officer: ODA 31 Team Leader, 1st Battalion, 10th Special Forces Group (Airborne), Bad Tölz, West Germany, April 1985 – June 1987.
* Project Officer, Training Exercises, SOCOM, Fort Bragg, 1987–1989.
* S3, Training Group Support Battalion, 1993–1994. Retired August 1994.

## In His Words

In 2009 Ulricson answered five written questions by hand, returning them with the note: "You owe me nothing! Just keeping the Mike Force alive is enough appreciation & happiness. Keep up the good work!" His answers are transcribed verbatim below.

### What was your motivation to volunteer for Special Forces?

"What motivated me initially was the physical & mental challenge of being S.F. Also, being w/ the best trained soldiers in the Army. I spent 1yr. in Tng. Grp. & served w/ the most professional NCO's in the Army… dedicated, professional motivated, pride & never whining about duty to our country. They realized their risk & went the extra mile. Some of these men came home in body bags. There are some things worth dying for…"

### What did you like/dislike about being on an A-Team?

"Again, serving w/ the BEST. Workg w/ our allies, e.g. DUTCH, CANADIAN, SAS & GERMANS. I liked the physical & mental challenge. The diversity in the mission… operations from living in snow caves, jumping out of MC-130s, living in the jungle w/ Rhade, rock climbing, wpns tng, meetg people, never bored, a sense of accomplishing the mission(s), and surviving all of this. I have approximately 2x dozen friends names carved on The Wall. Life is precious."

"Dog & pony shows for VIPs. Warm beer. Learning of a close friends KIA. The medic. Rats crawling on you at nite… leeches. Dumb people; that could get others killed. People that give up."

### What did you like/dislike about being with a Mike Force?

"The Mike Force was the Montagnards. Totally loyal to our mission & to us. Did not run away from enemy contact. Love to party after contact operations. Watch cowboy shows. Play volleyball. They knew the "bush". Took care of me. The best fighters!"

"I liked & enjoyed my complete tour w/ the M.F. If not in the bush I spent my free time w/ the Rhade; socializing, training, eating, drinking, partying – understanding them, as well as, they understand me."

"I honestly cannot recall any time I would put in the "dislike" category. (I'm sure there were some)."

### Any interesting stories you would care to share about training, camp life, patrols, operations, etc?

"My entire 6 mos w/ the Rhade I could write an action story/novel. Following is a list of stuff I remember & share w/ you.

– watching John Wayne movies – cowboy  
– drinking & partying w/ them. I would buy gin at PX on sale  
– playing volley ball… combat style  
– lookg at TIME/LIFE magazines together & explaining U.S. customs, values, culture  
– Chep "Rhade Co Cdr" – appropriatg a mattress for him.  
– Being invited & participating in 3x black rock ceremony w/ village elders. And receiving a Montagnard bracelet.  
– Crossing rivers & throwing in concussion grenades to get fish dinner that evening. Cut in two, stuffed in a canteen cup, heated w/ C-4 provides a yummy hot meal  
– crossing bomb craters by walking single file over the crater on a fallen tree. The one that falls in is wet & we all get a good belly laugh at of it.  
– Rhade had a great sense for enjoying life & realized "their" day w/ the Americans will end & probably not the way they want.

My time in the Mike Force & with the Rhade will be forever etched in my being for my lifetime."
