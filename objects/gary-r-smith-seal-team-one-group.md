## Grouping Details

### OD Jungle Jacket
* 1968-dated OD tropical combat jacket, sleeves shortened
* Theater-made "SMITH" name tape and "U.S. NAVY" tape
* Theater-made South Vietnamese jump wings above the name tape
* Navy SEAL Trident embroidered on sateen cloth above the "U.S. NAVY" tape
* Vietnamese-made printed LDNN patch in a plastic pocket hanger with a leather tab

### ERDL Jungle Jackets
**Jacket 1**
* 1968-dated ERDL tropical combat jacket ("OP Top")
* Theater-made "SMITH" name tape only

**Jacket 2**
* 1968-dated ERDL tropical combat jacket
* Theater-made "SMITH, G.R." name tape and "U.S. NAVY" tape
* Navy SEAL Trident embroidered on poly-blend twill

**Jacket 3**
* 1968-dated ERDL tropical combat jacket, short sleeves
* "SMITH" name tape and "U.S. NAVY" tape embroidered on cotton webbing
* Navy SEAL Trident embroidered on poly-blend twill

### ERDL Boonie Hat
* ERDL camouflage boonie hat
* Early wide (3.25") brim
* Label and chin cord removed

### ERDL Jungle Trousers
* Poplin (pre-rip-stop) ERDL tropical combat trousers
* Theater-made "SMITH" name tape on the rear pocket
* No DSA contract number on the label, circa 1967
* Tailored cuffs
* Period repair to the seat

### OD Jungle Trousers
* 1967-dated OD tropical combat trousers
* Printed "SMITH" name tape on the rear pocket

### OD Utility Uniform
**Shirt**
* 1972-dated OD utility shirt, short sleeves
* Theater-made "SMITH, G.R." name tape and "U.S. NAVY" tape
* Navy SEAL Trident embroidered on poly-blend twill

**Trousers**
* 1972-dated OD utility trousers
* "Smith" penned on the label

### Dress Blue Uniform
**Jumper**
* Wool dress blue jumper
* SEAL Team 1 shoulder scroll
* UDT patch
* Battle Efficiency "E" patch
* First Class Radioman (RM1) rating badge with two service stripes
* "Smith, Gary R" and his Social Security number penned on a white tag
* "GRS" stenciled in white ink

**Trousers**
* Wool dress blue trousers, size 30R
* "Smith, Gary R" and his Social Security number penned on the issue label
* "GRS" stenciled in white ink
* Significant repair to the seat

### UDT Coral Booties
* 1969-dated canvas coral booties
* Early concave vent grommets
* Zinc eyelets

## Military Service

* Enlisted in the U.S. Navy at Dallas, Texas, in January 1964. His records list Wichita Falls, Texas, as his place of entry.
* Radioman training at San Diego, 1964. Underwater Demolition Training at Coronado, graduating with UDT Training Class 36, 1965.
* UDT TWELVE, 1965–1967, qualified as a diver and parachutist.
* SEAL Team ONE from 1967, with deployments to Vietnam through Naval Support Activity Saigon in 1967–68 and 1970. Reenlisted in Saigon, June 19, 1970.
* Later service included Explosive Ordnance Disposal school at Indian Head, Maryland, the U.S. Army Sergeants Major Academy, Class 17 (1981), and further tours with SEAL Team ONE and UDT TWELVE.
* Transferred to the Fleet Reserve as a Master Chief Radioman (RMCM), April 30, 1984.
* Decorations per his service records: Purple Heart; Bronze Star (4); Navy Commendation Medal with Combat "V" (2); Combat Action Ribbon; Presidential Unit Citation (2); Navy Unit Commendation (5); Meritorious Unit Commendation (3); Vietnam Service Medal (5); RVN Cross of Gallantry (3); RVN Staff Service Medal, 2nd Class (2); National Defense Service Medal; Good Conduct Medal (4).
* Author of *Death in the Jungle*, *Death in the Delta* and *Master Chief*, his three-volume *Diary of a Navy SEAL*, and of *DemoMen*.
* After retiring he served as a Deputy Sheriff in Jack County, Texas. He died March 23, 2009, and is buried at [Wesley Chapel Cemetery, Jacksboro, Texas](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/54742144/gary-roger-smith). His government headstone reads "RMCM US NAVY, VIETNAM" and records the Bronze Star Medal and Purple Heart.

## Provenance

* Acquired by the author in the late 1990s from a collector in Moran, Texas, who found the group at a local gun show.
* Smith lived in Jack County, Texas, not far from Moran.
* Attribution verified: the Social Security number inked inside the uniforms matches the number in his official Navy personnel records, obtained from the National Personnel Records Center in 2004.
