## Object Details

* U.S. Army first-pattern tropical combat jacket in olive green cotton poplin.
* Cut-edge 173rd Airborne Brigade shoulder sleeve insignia on both sleeves.
* Specialist Four rank insignia with visible ghost outlines of previously attached Private E-2 insignia.
* Theater-made embroidered "PAYNE" name tape.
* Printed "U.S. ARMY" tape.
* Hand-embroidered U.S. parachutist wings with mustard-colored staining.
* Theater-made Combat Infantryman Badge (CIB).
* South Vietnamese silk-woven ARVN Jump Status pocket patch featuring a gold winged dagger on a black shield.
* Faded "PAY" handwritten in marker inside the jacket.

### Provenance

* Attributed to Specialist Four Jesse James Payne, U.S. Army, 173rd Airborne Brigade.
* Served in the U.S. Army from April 14, 1965, to March 20, 1967.
* Deployed to Vietnam in March 1966, serving initially with Company F and later Company B, 2nd Battalion (Airborne), 503rd Infantry Regiment.
* Awarded the Purple Heart, Combat Infantryman Badge, and Parachutist Badge, as documented in National Archives personnel records.
* Identified by name on the 173rd Airborne Brigade's [Operation Junction City jump roster](https://web.archive.org/web/20030624223227/http://173airborne.com/manifest.htm) for the February 22, 1967, combat parachute assault near Katum, recorded in General Orders 444, March 12, 1967.
* Acquired through eBay auction on December 21, 2008

### Supporting Documentation

* National Archives military personnel records for Jesse James Payne.
* Record of Assignments documenting service with the 2nd Battalion (Airborne), 503rd Infantry Regiment.
* 173rd Airborne Brigade Operation Junction City jump roster.
