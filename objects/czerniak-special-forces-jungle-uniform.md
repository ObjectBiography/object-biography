## Object Details

* First-pattern Coat, Man's, Combat, Tropical, with matching trousers, both dated 1963.
* Laundry stamp "4511-C" on the coat, matching the last four digits of Czerniak's Army service number and the initial of his surname, as recorded in his National Archives service records.
* Special Forces shoulder sleeve insignia with Airborne tab on the left shoulder.
* Master sergeant chevrons on both sleeves.
* Cloth Combat Infantryman Badge and parachutist wings.
* "CZERNIAK" name tape and black and gold "U.S. ARMY" tape.
* "CZ", an abbreviation of his surname, written in marker on the jacket's instruction label. The same abbreviation appears on the trousers' red laundry tag, which reads "26CZ24".
* The master sergeant chevrons predate Czerniak's promotion to sergeant major in June 1968. He held the rank of master sergeant from January 1963, a span that includes his 1964–65 Okinawa and Vietnam service and the first months of his 1968 Vietnam tour.
* The Combat Infantryman Badge places the jacket's configuration after his first combat service. His records show no Korean War combat service, so the badge was earned in Vietnam, most likely during his 1964 temporary duty tour as team sergeant of Detachment A1/214A at Plei Djereng.

### Service History

* Born January 13, 1929, in Minnesota.
* Entered the Army at Fort Snelling, Minnesota, on October 2, 1946, at age 17.
* Attended Army Officer Candidate School in 1951 and completed the Basic Airborne and Jumpmaster courses at The Infantry School the same year.
* Served as an Army recruiter in Minneapolis, 1960–1962.
* February 1962: Joined Special Forces as an infantry operations and intelligence specialist, Company C, 5th Special Forces Group (Airborne), Fort Bragg. Promoted to master sergeant in January 1963.
* March 1964–July 1965: Operations sergeant with the 1st Special Forces Group (Airborne) on Okinawa, credited with 16 months of overseas service in Okinawa and the Republic of Vietnam.
* June 19–December 21, 1964: Team sergeant of Detachment A1/214A, deployed from the 1st Special Forces Group on temporary duty to the CIDG camp at Plei Djereng, Pleiku Province, in Vietnam's II Corps Tactical Zone. The camp's strike force was drawn from the Jarai and Rhade peoples.
* August 1965: Operations sergeant, Company B, 7th Special Forces Group (Airborne), Fort Bragg.
* January 12, 1968–January 2, 1969: Detachment sergeant major, Company D, 5th Special Forces Group (Airborne), Vietnam. Radix Press's records place him as sergeant major of Detachment B-41 at Moc Hoa.
* Promoted to sergeant major on June 11, 1968, announced in *The Green Beret*, the monthly magazine of the 5th Special Forces Group, in its June 1968 issue (p. 24).
* 1969: Detachment sergeant major with Companies E and A, 7th Special Forces Group (Airborne), Fort Bragg.
* 1969–1975: Chief instructor at the Special Forces School, U.S. Army Institute for Military Assistance, Fort Bragg.
* Retired as a sergeant major (E-9) on May 31, 1975, at Fort Bragg, after nearly 29 years of service.
* Died August 9, 1999, in Fayetteville, North Carolina. He is memorialized in the Special Forces Association Memorial Garden, Fayetteville, where his government marker reads "CSM US ARMY, WWII KOREA VIETNAM" and records the Bronze Star Medal.

* Bronze Star Medal.

* Army Commendation Medal.
* Combat Infantryman Badge.
* Parachutist Badge.
* Good Conduct Medal (six awards).
* World War II Victory Medal.
* Army of Occupation Medal (Germany).
* National Defense Service Medal with oak leaf cluster.
* Vietnam Service Medal.
* Republic of Vietnam Cross of Gallantry with Palm.
* Republic of Vietnam Campaign Medal.
* Presidential Unit Citation and Meritorious Unit Commendation.

### Military Schools

* Automotive Mechanic, Tracked Vehicle Course, and NCO Academy, Fort Knox, Kentucky, 1950.
* Officer Candidate School (24 weeks), Army General School, and Associate Company Officer Infantry Course (15 weeks), U.S. Army Infantry School, 1951.
* Basic Airborne and Jumpmaster courses, The Infantry School, 1951.
* Seventh Army CBR School and Troop Information and Education courses, 1952.
* Recruiting and Career Counselor Course, The Adjutant General's School, 1960.
* Infantry Operations and Intelligence Specialist (Special Forces), U.S. Army Special Warfare School, 1962.
* Basic Vietnamese Terminology, Sukiran Army Education Center, Okinawa, 1964.
* Intelligence Analyst (Special Forces), U.S. Army Intelligence School, 1965.
* NCO Orientation Course, U.S. Army Combat Surveillance School, 1966.
* Installation Intelligence Course, U.S. Army Intelligence School, 1967.

### About Detachment B-41

* Moc Hoa was established in 1963 as Special Forces camp A-414 and upgraded in 1965 to a B detachment, B-41. In 1966 it became home to the IV Corps Mobile Strike Force (Mike Force).
