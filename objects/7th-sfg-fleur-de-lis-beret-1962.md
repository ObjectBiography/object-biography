## Object Details

* Rifle green wool beret made in Toronto by Dorothea Knitting Mills under the Fleur de Lis brand, dated 1962.
* Interior maker's stamp: Fleur de Lis / 6⅞ 1962 / Dorothea Knitting Mills Limited / Toronto, with a separate "Made in Canada" mark.
* 7th Special Forces Group beret flash cut from red satin, with turned-under edges stitched to the beret, and larger than the later machine-embroidered flash. Materials such as felt or satin were often used for flashes before the machine-embroidered flash was formalized.
* Early two-piece Special Forces distinctive unit insignia of the type collectors call the "skull harp."
* Black leather sweatband and dark cotton lining.
* Size 6⅞.
* No names, laundry numbers, or other markings.

### Provenance

* In the author's collection since the late 1990s or early 2000s; the source of acquisition is not recorded.
* No attribution to a wearer accompanies the beret.
