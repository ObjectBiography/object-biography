## Object Details

* Theater-made bush hat constructed from U.S. military camouflage parachute material.
* Heavily sun-faded green camouflage fabric, with more pronounced fading across the crown and brim.
* Chin cord made from 550 paracord.

### Provenance

* Worn by LT Charles B. "Chuck" Darnell Jr. during his service with Operation White Star in Laos.
* Assigned to Detachment FA-7A at Muang Sing, Laos, from August 6, 1961, to January 1, 1962, serving as detachment executive officer/commanding officer.
* Operation White Star was a covert U.S. Army Special Forces mission supporting anti-communist forces in Laos before the large-scale American military commitment in Vietnam.
* Awarded the Combat Infantryman Badge during his service in Laos.
* Military author and researcher Jason M. Hardy specifically identified this bush hat as having been issued to Darnell during his White Star service.
* Acquired directly from Charles B. Darnell Jr. by Hardy at the 50th Special Forces convention in Fayetteville, North Carolina, in spring 2002.
* Acquired from Hardy by the present collection later in 2002.

### Supporting Documentation

* Signed provenance letter from Jason M. Hardy.
* Copies of Darnell's military service records.
* Stephen Sherman, *Who's Who from Hotfoot/White Star*, which identifies Darnell's rank, detachment, duty position, and dates of service.
* Jason M. Hardy and Stephen Sherman, *CIB/CMB and VN Jump Wing Awards to Special Forces in Vietnam*, which documents Darnell's Combat Infantryman Badge.
