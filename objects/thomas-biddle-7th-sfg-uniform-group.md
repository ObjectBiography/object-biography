## Group Details

* Three U.S. Army uniforms attributed to First Sergeant Thomas Coay Biddle, a veteran of the 7th Special Forces Group (Airborne), whose military career spanned 1986–2015.
* The grouping consists of one woodland camouflage Battle Dress Uniform (BDU) coat and two Universal Camouflage Pattern (UCP) Army Combat Uniform (ACU) coats.
* Biddle served nearly three decades in the U.S. Army, with service in the Persian Gulf and Afghanistan. His qualifications included military freefall parachuting, military diving, and master parachutist certification.
* The woodland BDU is a non-ripstop, temperate-weather coat bearing Sergeant First Class (E-7) collar insignia, Master Parachutist Badge, Military Freefall Parachutist Badge, and Special Operations Diver Badge.
* An interior property stamp reads “PROPERTY OF U.S. CIF PANAMA,” indicating an association with the Central Issue Facility in Panama.
* Biddle died in a diving accident on April 7, 2016, less than a year after retiring from the Army.

### Provenance

* Acquired by a Florida-based reseller, reportedly directly from Biddle's estate following his death in 2016.
* Ownership attribution is supported by the name markings on the uniforms, the seller's account of their estate origin, personalized materials kept by the seller, and independently located biographical records.

### Published Accounts

* A July 2016 memorial post on the [ScubaBoard diving forum](https://www.scubaboard.com/community/threads/military-man-diving-accident.531031/), citing a Special Forces Association publication, describes him as a retired Master Sergeant and a 25-year member of U.S. Army Special Forces.
* The same account identifies him as a graduate of the Special Forces Combat Diver program, a dive supervisor and dive medicine technician, a HALO (military freefall) jumper, and a master parachutist.
* It reports that the fatal diving accident occurred in Panama City.

### Additional History

* The Panama Central Issue Facility marking raises questions about Biddle's service in Central America, including whether he participated in Operation Just Cause (1989) or subsequent operations in Panama. No direct connection to the operation has been established.
