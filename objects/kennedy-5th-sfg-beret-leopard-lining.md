## The Object

* U.S. Army Special Forces shaved green wool beret belonging to Freddie Arden Kennedy, a career Army officer and Vietnam veteran.
* Features a fully machine-embroidered flash of the 5th Special Forces Group (Airborne), fitted with silver captain's rank insignia.
* Interior retains a cloth name tape marked KENNEDY.
* According to Kennedy's wife, who personally wrote the original eBay auction description, the leopard-print cotton lining was installed in Nha Trang, South Vietnam, in February 1968.
* Kennedy's wife identified the beret as one of three worn by her husband. It is not known whether this particular beret was worn during his earlier Vietnam service.

### Military Service

* Entered the U.S. Army on June 6, 1956, serving until May 14, 1958, before returning to active service on July 16, 1962.
* Served with the 5th Special Forces Group (Airborne) during the Vietnam War.
* Stephen Sherman's CIB/CMB and VN Jump Wing Awards to Special Forces in Vietnam (Radix Press, p. 63) identifies Kennedy as a second lieutenant assigned to Detachment C-4, 5th Special Forces Group.
* Kennedy received his first Combat Infantryman Badge under award order 5S/66-0053, dated February 26, 1966.
* May 1968–May 1969: Served as S-2 (Intelligence Officer), Company C, 5th Special Forces Group (Airborne), in Vietnam. This assignment is documented in his official personnel record and corroborates information provided through personal correspondence with Stephen Sherman.
* 1969–1970: Served as an intelligence officer with the Army's Intelligence Control Group in Washington, D.C.
* 1970–1971: Served in Thailand in military intelligence and psychological operations assignments.
* 1972–1973: Commanded the 542nd Military Intelligence Detachment.
* 1976–1978: Held intelligence staff assignments with the 7th Infantry Division at Fort Ord, California, including brigade S-2 and assistant G-2.
* 1979–1981: Served with the Defense Nuclear Agency in intelligence and special staff assignments.
* 1981–1983: Served on the Army General Staff in Washington, D.C., including duty as an Operations Security Staff Officer.
* Kennedy retired from active military service on September 30, 1983, at Fort Myer, Virginia, having attained the rank of lieutenant colonel.

### Provenance

* The beret remained in the possession of Kennedy's family following his military service.
* In February 1999, Kennedy's wife offered the beret for sale on eBay.
* She personally authored the auction description, identifying the beret as one of three worn by her husband.
* Her description specifically documented the leopard-print lining and its installation in Nha Trang in February 1968.
* The beret was acquired directly through this auction, with the original listing preserved.
* Subsequent research produced Kennedy's military service records from the National Personnel Records Center in 2009.
* Additional documentation of Kennedy's Vietnam service was obtained through Stephen Sherman's published research and personal correspondence.
